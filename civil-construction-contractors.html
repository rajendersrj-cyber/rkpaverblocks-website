Civil Construction & Infrastructure Works

R K Paver Blocks & Cement Products manufactures and supplies precast cement products and also undertakes civil construction and infrastructure works. We consider project requirements for roads and WBM, paving and parking, boundary walls, brickwork, kerb and drainage works, and other civil construction activities.

Our Civil Construction & Contracting Services

We undertake suitable civil construction and infrastructure works according to project requirements, specifications and agreed scope. The services below describe the types of work for which project enquiries are welcome.

Roads & WBM Works

Enquiries are welcome for road-related civil works, water-bound macadam (WBM) works and associated site development activities. The scope is assessed according to the project specifications and site requirements.

Paving & Parking Works

Paver block laying and paved-area development for suitable parking areas, industrial premises, commercial properties, residential developments and other external spaces.

Our manufacturing range includes interlocking paver blocks in different designs and specifications. Product supply and laying work can be discussed together where appropriate. Explore our paver blocks .

Boundary Wall Construction

Boundary and compound wall work for suitable plots, industrial premises, commercial properties and other developments. Requirements may involve precast boundary wall systems or other specified wall construction methods, depending on the agreed scope.

Explore our precast boundary walls .

Brickwork & Masonry

Brick walls, masonry and associated civil works according to project drawings, specifications and agreed requirements.

Kerb Stone & Drainage Works

Kerb stone installation, saucer drain work and associated external development activities for suitable road, parking and site-development projects. Precast product supply can also be discussed according to project requirements.

Explore kerb stones and saucer drains .

Other Civil Construction Works

We also welcome enquiries for other civil construction and infrastructure activities that fall within our execution capabilities. The feasibility, scope, materials, specifications and project requirements are reviewed before a quotation is provided.

Manufacturing Capability Alongside Civil Contracting

Manufacturing remains a core part of R K Paver Blocks & Cement Products. Alongside our civil construction activities, we manufacture and supply paver blocks, kerb stones, saucer drains, precast boundary walls, cover blocks, trench covers, manhole covers and other cement products.

Where the project scope requires products from our range, customers can discuss product supply and relevant site works together. Requirements, quantities, specifications, delivery and execution arrangements are considered for each project.
