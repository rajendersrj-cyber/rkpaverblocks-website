MANUFACTURING FACILITY

New Manufacturing Facility in Gharaunda, Haryana

R K Paver Blocks & Cement Products has expanded its manufacturing operations with a new production facility on Dinger Majra Road, Gharaunda, Haryana.

The facility strengthens our manufacturing and supply capabilities for paver blocks, interlocking pavers, kerb stones, trench covers, manhole covers, cover blocks, readymade/precast boundary walls and other precast cement products.

The Gharaunda facility is part of our wider manufacturing setup, with additional manufacturing operations also located in Gharaunda and Karnal.

PAVER BLOCKS

Wide Range of Paver Blocks & Interlocking Pavers

Our paver block range includes different shapes, colours, patterns and specifications for residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure applications.

Available designs include Zebra, Dumbbell, Milano, Cosmic, V-Shape, Cobble, Grass Pavers, 8×8 and 8×4 pavers, along with other designs based on project requirements.

Paver blocks are available in 60 mm, 80 mm and 100 mm specifications depending on customer and project requirements.
