Do you manufacture RCC trench covers in Panipat?

Yes. We manufacture RCC trench covers (also called precast trench covers or concrete trench cover slabs) and supply across Panipat, Karnal and nearby areas.

What sizes are available in precast trench covers?

Common sizes include 1x1 ft, 1.5x1.5 ft and 2x2 ft. Custom sizes can also be manufactured as per project requirement.

Do you provide heavy duty trench covers?

Yes. We provide light, medium and heavy duty trench covers depending on load requirement and installation location.

RCC trench covers are used in industrial drains, commercial drainage, factory trenches, construction sites and utility trenches.
