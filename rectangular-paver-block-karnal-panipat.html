8X4 Rectangular Paver Blocks – Manufacturer in Karnal & Panipat

Our 8x4 rectangular paver blocks are precast interlocking concrete pavers laid in a traditional brick-bond pattern, giving a clean and durable finish for driveways, internal colony roads, footpaths and commercial walkways. Manufactured as cement concrete paving tiles, these rectangular pavers are supplied across Karnal, Panipat, Gharaunda, Sonipat, Delhi, Samalkha and Shamli for both residential and institutional projects.

Where 8X4 Rectangular Paver Blocks are Used

  • Residential driveways
  • Colony & society internal roads
  • Footpaths & walkways
  • Institutional campuses

Specifications

  • Available Thickness: 40 mm, 60 mm, 80 mm
  • Material: High-strength Cement Concrete
  • Concrete Grade: M30 / M35 as per requirement
  • Supply Areas: Gharaunda, Panipat, Karnal, Sonipat, Delhi, Shamli, Samalkha, Taraori, Kurukshetra, Nilokheri, Indri
