About Us

R K Paver Blocks & Cement Products is a manufacturer and supplier of interlocking paver blocks, paving tiles and RCC precast cement products.

We manufacture and supply precast cement products including RCC boundary walls, kerb stones, trench covers, slabs, manhole covers and interlocking paver blocks across Panipat, Karnal, Samalkha, Taraori, Sonipat, Kurukshetra, Nilokheri, Delhi, Shamli and Muzaffarnagar.
