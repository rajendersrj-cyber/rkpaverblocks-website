About Us

R K Paver Blocks & Cement Products is a manufacturer and supplier of interlocking paver blocks, paving tiles and RCC precast cement products. We have supplied and executed projects across industrial, township, and infrastructure developments.

We manufacture and supply precast cement products including RCC boundary walls, kerb stones, RCC Precast Trench Covers, RCC Slabs, RCC Manhole Covers, cover blocks and interlocking paver blocks across Panipat, Karnal, Samalkha, Taraori, Sonipat, Kurukshetra, Nilokheri, Delhi, Shamli and Muzaffarnagar.

Frequently Asked Questions

What precast cement products do you manufacture?

We manufacture a wide range of precast cement products including RCC boundary wall panels, interlocking paver blocks, paving tiles, kerb stones, cover blocks, drain covers and manhole covers.

Which locations do you supply precast concrete products to?

We supply precast cement products to Panipat, Karnal, Samalkha, Taraori, Sonipat, Kurukshetra, Nilokheri, Delhi, Shamli and Muzaffarnagar, catering to industrial, residential, commercial and infrastructure projects.

Do you manufacture precast products at your own factory?

Yes, all precast cement products are manufactured at our own facility using controlled processes to ensure consistent quality and durability.

Are your precast concrete products suitable for large projects?

Yes, our precast RCC products are suitable for industrial, commercial, township and infrastructure projects requiring reliable and scalable supply.
