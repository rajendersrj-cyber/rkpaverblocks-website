FAQ – Precast Cement Products in Panipat, Karnal

What are precast cement products?

Precast cement products are RCC / concrete items manufactured in a controlled factory setting and supplied to site for fast installation. This includes boundary wall panels, paver blocks, kerb stones, trench covers and more.

Which precast products do you supply in Panipat and Karnal?

We supply readymade precast boundary walls, interlocking paver blocks, paving tiles, kerb stones, saucer drains, trench covers, drain covers, manhole covers and cover blocks in Panipat, Karnal and nearby areas.
