V Shaped Paver Blocks – Manufacturer in Karnal & Panipat

The V shape paver block, laid in a chevron pattern, interlocks in multiple directions for excellent resistance to shifting under load. This interlocking concrete paver is well suited for driveways, parking areas and commercial forecourts across Karnal, Panipat, Gharaunda, Sonipat, Delhi and Samalkha. Manufactured to standard IS specifications in 60mm and 80mm thickness for durable, long-lasting paving.

Where V Shaped Paver Blocks are Used

  • Driveways & forecourts
  • Parking areas
  • Petrol pump & showroom paving
  • Commercial complexes

Specifications

  • Available Thickness: 40 mm, 60 mm, 80 mm
  • Material: High-strength Cement Concrete
  • Concrete Grade: M30 / M35 as per requirement
