Hexagon Paver Block – Manufacturer in Karnal & Panipat

Our hexagon paver blocks are precast interlocking concrete pavers designed with a six-sided honeycomb shape that locks tightly with adjoining blocks, giving a stable, low-maintenance surface. This hexagonal interlocking tile pattern is widely used for parking lots, footpaths, park walkways, petrol pump flooring and residential driveways across Karnal, Panipat, Gharaunda, Sonipat, Delhi, Samalkha, Shamli and Kurukshetra. Being a cement concrete paver block, it offers high compressive strength, good skid resistance and easy replacement of individual pieces without disturbing the surrounding surface.

Where Hexagon Paver Block are Used

  • Parking areas & driveways
  • Garden and park pathways
  • Petrol pump & showroom flooring
  • Footpaths and walkways

Specifications

  • Available Thickness: 40 mm, 60 mm, 80 mm
  • Material: High-strength Cement Concrete
  • Concrete Grade: M30 / M35 as per requirement
  • Supply Areas: Gharaunda, Panipat, Karnal, Sonipat, Delhi, Shamli, Samalkha, Taraori, Kurukshetra, Nilokheri, Indri
